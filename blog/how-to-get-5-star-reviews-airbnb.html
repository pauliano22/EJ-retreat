How to Get More 5-Star Reviews on Airbnb

November 02, 2026 6 min read STR Strategy
```html

Your Airbnb listing's review score isn't just a vanity metric — it's the single most powerful factor driving your booking rate, your search ranking, and ultimately your revenue. Hosts who consistently earn 5-star reviews fill their calendars faster, command higher nightly rates, and earn Superhost status that compounds their visibility over time. If you're wondering how to get 5-star reviews on Airbnb, the good news is that it comes down to a repeatable system — not luck.

Want to know if your property is earning what it should? Underperforming reviews cost you real money every month.

Set the Right Expectations Before Guests Even Arrive

The most common reason guests leave 4-star reviews isn't because something went wrong — it's because something didn't match what they expected. Professional STR managers know that the guest experience begins the moment someone reads your listing, not when they walk through the door.

Write an Honest, Detailed Listing Description

Your photos and description need to tell the complete, accurate story of your property. Highlight your genuine strengths — the cozy fireplace, the walkable neighborhood, the brand-new kitchen — but don't hide limitations like street noise, a steep driveway, or a quirky water heater. Guests who know what to expect are far more forgiving, and guests who feel surprised by something negative will almost always mention it in their review. Transparency isn't weakness; it's one of the most effective tools for learning how to get 5-star reviews on Airbnb.

Send a Pre-Arrival Message That Makes Guests Feel Prepared

Send a detailed pre-arrival message 24–48 hours before check-in. Include everything a guest needs to feel confident: parking instructions, access codes, Wi-Fi credentials, local restaurant recommendations, and a direct line to reach you. This single touchpoint dramatically reduces anxiety, eliminates preventable questions, and signals that you're a professional host who cares about their stay.

Pro Tip: Use Automated Messaging Thoughtfully

Automation tools like Hospitable, Hostfully, or Guesty allow you to send perfectly timed messages without manual effort. The key is to personalize each template with the guest's name and property-specific details — automated doesn't have to feel robotic.

Deliver a Spotless, Hotel-Quality In-Person Experience

Once your guest walks through the door, everything you wrote in your listing gets tested in real life. The properties that consistently earn 5-star reviews treat cleanliness and amenities like a non-negotiable standard — not an afterthought.

Invest in Professional Cleaning and a Strong Turnover Checklist

Cleanliness is the #1 category guests cite in negative reviews across every market in the country. A professional cleaning team with a detailed turnover checklist is one of the highest-ROI investments a vacation rental owner can make. Your checklist should cover:

  • Deep cleaning high-touch surfaces: Remotes, light switches, door handles, and countertops should be sanitized after every stay — not just wiped down.
  • Restocking consumables: Coffee, toiletries, paper products, and dish soap should be fully replenished before every guest arrives.
  • Staging the space: Towels folded neatly, throw pillows arranged, welcome card placed on the counter — these small visual details signal pride of ownership.
  • Checking every amenity: Test the TV remotes, confirm the Wi-Fi works, make sure every light bulb functions. Broken amenities that go unnoticed become scathing review bullets.
  • Inspecting linens closely: Any stain, tear, or odor on bedding or towels will immediately drop your cleanliness score, regardless of how perfect everything else is.

Add Thoughtful Touches That Guests Remember

The hosts who consistently earn 5-star reviews understand that guests don't just remember what they found — they remember how the space made them feel. Small, low-cost gestures create outsized emotional impressions. A handwritten welcome note, a locally sourced snack basket, a printed guide to the neighborhood's best hidden gems, or a bottle of wine for anniversary guests — these are the details that turn a 4-star stay into a glowing 5-star review and a direct repeat booking. At E&J Retreats, this kind of systematic hospitality is built into our standard operating procedures for every managed property nationwide, because we know the data proves it pays off.

Follow Up After Checkout to Encourage Reviews

Many guests genuinely enjoyed their stay but simply forget to leave a review. A friendly, non-pushy checkout message sent within a few hours of departure is one of the most effective ways to increase your review rate. Thank them for staying, let them know their review helps your small business, and mention that you'd love to host them again. Keep it warm and brief. Understanding how to get 5-star reviews on Airbnb is as much about follow-through as it is about the stay itself — and companies like E&J Retreats automate this step so no review opportunity ever slips through the cracks.

If a guest reaches out with a concern after checkout, respond quickly and resolve it graciously. Guests who feel heard after a minor issue often leave better reviews than those who had a flawless stay — because you demonstrated genuine care.
```
