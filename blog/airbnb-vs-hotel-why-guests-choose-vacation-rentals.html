Why Travelers Are Choosing Vacation Rentals Over Hotels

The growth of platforms like Airbnb and Vrbo isn't a trend — it's a fundamental shift in how Americans travel. Hotels offer consistency, but vacation rentals offer something more compelling: a sense of place, space, and authenticity that a standard hotel room simply can't replicate. Research consistently shows that families, remote workers, and group travelers overwhelmingly prefer STRs when given the choice, and those segments represent a massive share of total travel spending.

The Top Reasons Guests Pick STRs

Understanding the guest decision-making process helps property owners make smarter investment and management choices. Here's what drives the Airbnb vs hotel preference toward vacation rentals:

  • More space: Guests get separate living rooms, bedrooms, and often outdoor areas — ideal for families and groups who don't want to cram into a single hotel room.
  • Full kitchen access: The ability to cook meals is a top-cited factor, especially for longer stays and families with kids or dietary needs.
  • Cost efficiency for groups: Splitting one vacation rental among four to six people is almost always cheaper per person than booking multiple hotel rooms.
  • Privacy and flexibility: No front desk check-in windows, no noise from adjacent rooms, and no mandatory checkout by 11 AM.
  • Local authenticity: Travelers increasingly want to feel like they're living in a destination rather than passing through it — vacation rentals deliver that experience naturally.
  • Pet-friendly options: A significant percentage of American households travel with pets, and STRs offer far more pet-friendly inventory than most hotels.
  • Remote work compatibility: Dedicated workspaces, high-speed WiFi, and quiet environments make STRs the preferred choice for bleisure travelers combining business and leisure.

Tip: Amenities Drive Booking Decisions

Listings that specifically highlight a full kitchen, dedicated workspace, and pet-friendly policies consistently outperform comparable listings that leave those details vague. Don't assume guests will infer amenities — spell them out clearly in your listing description and photo captions.

What the Market Data Says About Guest Demand

The Airbnb vs hotel conversation isn't just anecdotal. Market analytics paint a clear picture of where traveler demand is headed. STR occupancy rates across major US leisure markets have remained competitive with — and in many cases exceeded — traditional hotel occupancy, even as hotel inventory has expanded. Average daily rates for vacation rentals have also climbed steadily, particularly for well-managed properties with strong reviews and professional photography.
