Case Study #1 Vacation Rental — Florida

Sitting at $0 for 3 months. First month with us: $12,000.

A property owner came to us after their rental sat completely empty for three months straight — zero bookings, zero revenue. The property itself was a goldmine: great location, solid amenities. The problem was entirely operational. They were on one platform, had no pricing strategy, and the listing was doing nothing to convert views into bookings.

What we did

  • Listed on 15+ booking platforms — had been on just one
  • Implemented our dynamic pricing system with real-time market data
  • Rewrote and fully optimized the listing — copy, photos, titles, amenity descriptions
  • Set up automated guest communication, cleaning coordination, and complete backend operations
$12,000
First month under E&J management

The property has been consistently booked ever since. What looked like a struggling asset was simply being mismanaged — the demand was always there. We just made the property visible, competitive, and fully set up to capture it.
